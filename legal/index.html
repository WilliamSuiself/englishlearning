Privacy Policy

Last Updated: July 16, 2025

Thank you for using English Learning App (hereinafter referred to as "the App"). We take your privacy and personal information protection seriously. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, store, and protect your information.

1. Information Collection

We may collect the following types of information:

  • Learning Data: Your learning progress, answer records, wrong answer records, and other learning-related data stored locally on your device.
  • Device Information: Basic device information such as model and OS version to optimize app performance.
  • Voice Data: If you use the speaking practice feature, voice data is processed locally and will not be uploaded to any server.
  • Usage Statistics: Anonymous usage statistics to help us improve the app experience.

2. Information Use

We use the collected information to:

  • Provide and maintain learning features
  • Track learning progress and calculate accuracy rates
  • Personalize learning experience
  • Improve app functionality and user experience
  • Resolve technical issues and provide customer support

3. Data Storage

Your learning data is primarily stored locally on your device (SQLite database). We do not store your personal learning data on cloud servers.

4. Information Sharing

We do not sell, trade, or otherwise transfer your personal information to external third parties. We only share information in the following circumstances:

  • With your explicit consent
  • When required by law or regulation
  • To protect our rights and property

5. Data Security

We employ industry-standard security measures to protect your information, including data encryption and access controls. However, please note that no data transmission or storage method is 100% secure.

6. Third-Party Services

The App may contain links to or integrations with third-party services. We are not responsible for the privacy practices of third-party services and recommend that you review their privacy policies.

7. Children's Privacy

This App is not intended for children under 13 years of age, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

8. Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Delete locally stored learning data at any time (by uninstalling the app or clearing app data)
  • Decline to provide non-essential information (though this may affect some features)

9. Privacy Policy Updates

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Updated policies will be posted on this page. We recommend that you review this page regularly to stay informed of any changes.
